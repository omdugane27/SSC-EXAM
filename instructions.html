Important Instructions

  • Read all examination instructions carefully before starting.
  • The examination timer will start when the examination begins.
  • Select only one option for each question.
  • You can navigate between questions using the question navigation controls.
  • You may review and change your answers before submitting the examination.
  • Do not refresh, close or leave the examination page during the examination.
  • Do not use unauthorized books, notes, mobile devices or other materials.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilator.
  • Submit the examination before the timer expires.
  • Once the examination is finally submitted, answers cannot be changed.
Important: If you experience any technical problem during the examination, immediately inform the invigilator. Do not attempt to restart the examination on your own.

Candidate Declaration

Examination access is valid only for the authorized candidate.
